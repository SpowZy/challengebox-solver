That gap is small, and reporting it as the headline would be dishonest: agreeing with a reference that was written from the same statement is not the same as passing a hidden test, and the reference can share a misreading with the candidates.

The number that a single prompt cannot produce at any sample count is the other one: **5/10** statements where two readings of the same sentence drove different behaviour on the same input. One prompt yields one reading and cannot disagree with itself, so its `Divergence` column is empty by construction, not by merit. Sampling it seven times varies the seed and leaves the reading fixed. Varying the reading is what buys the column, and the column is what tells a reader which answers to distrust.
